Expense Tracker is a simple, private way to log what you spend and see where your money goes.

FEATURES
• Add an expense in seconds: title, amount (NPR) and category
• Categories: Food, Transport, Bills, Shopping, Other
• See your total and per-category totals at a glance
• Filter by category and check this month's spending
• Delete entries you added by mistake

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline: no internet permission
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your data stays only on your phone

Privacy policy: https://sabin78910.github.io/privacy-policy/#expense-tracker
